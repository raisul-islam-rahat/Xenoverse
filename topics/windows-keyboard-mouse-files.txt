Question: How do copy, cut, paste, and undo shortcuts work?
Answer: English: Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy the selected item and paste a copy into another place. Ctrl+X cuts the selected item so it can be moved with Paste. Ctrl+Z undoes the most recent supported action.
Practise the task on a safe example, watch what changes, and repeat it until you can complete it without risking an important file.
Bangla: Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V selected item copy করে অন্য জায়গায় তার copy paste করে। Ctrl+X selected item cut করে, যাতে Paste দিয়ে অন্য জায়গায় move করা যায়। Ctrl+Z সর্বশেষ সমর্থিত কাজটি undo করে।
