Question: What is a dieline, and what do cut and fold lines mean?
Answer: English: Dieline is the approved production template showing cut, fold, bleed, glue, and safe areas. Trim or cut line marks where the printed packaging will be cut. Fold or score line marks where packaging material will bend.
Add this point to the project brief or production checklist, apply it in context, and review the result with the relevant client, printer, or approver.
Bangla: Dieline approved production template, যা cut, fold, bleed, glue ও safe area দেখায়। Trim or cut line printed packaging কোথায় cut হবে তা দেখায়। Fold or score line packaging material কোথায় bend হবে তা দেখায়।

Question: What are spot colors and print finishes?
Answer: English: Spot color uses a named premixed ink when the production plan requires controlled special color. Print finish adds a physical treatment such as varnish, lamination, foil, embossing, or debossing with printer-approved setup.
Add this point to the project brief or production checklist, apply it in context, and review the result with the relevant client, printer, or approver.

Question: How do Smart Object, perspective, and displacement mockups work differently?
Answer: English: Smart Object mockup lets flat artwork update inside a reusable photographic presentation scene. Perspective mockup transforms artwork so it follows the visible planes of an object. Displacement mockup warps artwork according to surface light and texture information.
Place both versions side by side at the real viewing size and choose according to the brief, audience, message, and production requirement.
Bangla: Smart Object mockup reusable photographic presentation scene-এর ভেতরে flat artwork update করতে দেয়। Perspective mockup artwork transform করে, যাতে object-এর visible plane follow করে। Displacement mockup surface light ও texture information অনুযায়ী artwork warp করে।
real viewing size-এ দুইটি version পাশাপাশি রাখুন এবং brief, audience, message ও production requirement অনুযায়ী choose করুন।

Question: How do prepress, printer specifications, and a production proof prevent expensive mistakes?
Answer: English: Prepress prepares and checks production files before they reach printing or manufacturing. Printer specification provides the required profile, PDF standard, bleed, marks, inks, resolution, separations, and finishing setup. Production proof confirms content, color expectation, dimensions, codes, finishing, and construction before the full run.
