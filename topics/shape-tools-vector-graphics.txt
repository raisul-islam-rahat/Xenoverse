Question: What makes a vector graphic different from a raster image?

Question: How do Combine, Subtract, Intersect, and Exclude build custom shapes?
Answer: English: Combine Shapes adds vector components into one combined shape area. Subtract Front Shape cuts the front vector component out of the shape behind it. Intersect Shape Areas keeps only the overlap between vector components. Exclude Overlapping Shapes removes overlapping regions while keeping non-overlapping vector areas.
Start with the default settings, change one control at a time, and inspect the result at both a useful zoom and the final viewing size.
Bangla: Combine Shapes vector component এক combined shape area-তে যোগ করে। Subtract Front Shape সামনের vector component দিয়ে পেছনের shape কেটে বাদ দেয়। Intersect Shape Areas vector component-এর শুধু overlap রাখে। Exclude Overlapping Shapes overlapping region সরিয়ে non-overlapping vector area রাখে।
default setting দিয়ে শুরু করুন, একবারে একটি control বদলান এবং useful zoom ও final viewing size—দুই জায়গায় result দেখুন।
