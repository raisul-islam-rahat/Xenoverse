Question: Why should I paint on a blank or clipped layer instead of directly on the photo?
Answer: English: Paint on a blank layer keeps brush marks separate from the original image for safer editing. Clipped painting layer keeps painted color inside the visible content of the layer below.
Use this as a workflow checkpoint before committing the edit. Paint on a new layer when possible, watch opacity and flow, and do not install brushes from an untrusted source.
Bangla: Paint on a blank layer safer editing-এর জন্য brush mark-কে original image থেকে আলাদা রাখে। Clipped painting layer painted color-কে নিচের layer-এর visible content-এর মধ্যে রাখে।
