Question: Why should sharpening be checked at 100 percent view and applied subtly?
Answer: English: 100 percent view shows one image pixel per screen pixel and is useful for judging many sharpening artifacts. Subtle local correction uses several low-strength passes instead of one heavy stroke for a natural result.
Use this as a workflow checkpoint before committing the edit. Build effects slowly; too much blur, sharpening, saturation, or dodge and burn quickly makes an image look damaged.
Bangla: 100 percent view এক image pixel-কে এক screen pixel হিসেবে দেখায় এবং অনেক sharpening artifact বিচার করতে useful। Subtle local correction natural result-এর জন্য এক heavy stroke-এর বদলে কয়েকটি low-strength pass ব্যবহার করে।
edit commit করার আগে এটিকে workflow checkpoint হিসেবে ব্যবহার করুন। effect ধীরে build করুন; অতিরিক্ত blur, sharpening, saturation বা dodge and burn image দ্রুত damaged দেখায়।
