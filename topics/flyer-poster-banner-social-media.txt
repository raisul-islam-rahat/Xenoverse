Question: What should a campaign brief define about the objective, offer, and call to action?
Answer: English: Campaign brief defines the goal, audience, offer, message, formats, channels, deadline, and approval owner. Campaign objective states what the audience should know, feel, or do after seeing the design. Offer states the product, service, event, benefit, or promotion being presented. Call to action tells the viewer the next step, such as buy, register, visit, call, or learn more.
Apply it through the chapter project: turn one campaign brief into a flyer, poster, web banner, square social post, and vertical story while keeping one clear visual system.
Bangla: Campaign brief goal, audience, offer, message, format, channel, deadline ও approval owner নির্ধারণ করে। Campaign objective design দেখার পর audience কী জানবে, feel করবে বা করবে তা জানায়। Offer present করা product, service, event, benefit বা promotion জানায়। Call to action viewer-কে buy, register, visit, call বা learn more-এর মতো next step জানায়।

Question: How are web banners, feed posts, and vertical stories designed differently?
Answer: English: Web banner fits a message and action into a wide or compact screen placement. Social media post uses platform-appropriate dimensions and mobile-readable content for a feed. Vertical story design places key content inside safe zones for a tall mobile screen and interface overlays.
