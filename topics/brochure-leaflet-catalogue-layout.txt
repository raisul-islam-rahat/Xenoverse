Question: What is the difference between a brochure, leaflet, and catalogue?
Answer: English: Brochure presents structured information across panels or pages for promotion, explanation, or sales. Leaflet is usually a small printed sheet that may be flat, single-sided, double-sided, or folded. Catalogue organizes many products, services, or items with repeated information and navigation.
Apply it through the chapter project: plan a short brochure, prepare its images in Photoshop, build the page layout in a page-layout application, and produce a proof PDF.
Bangla: Brochure promotion, explanation বা sales-এর জন্য panel বা page জুড়ে structured information দেয়। Leaflet সাধারণত small printed sheet, যা flat, single-sided, double-sided বা folded হতে পারে। Catalogue repeated information ও navigation-সহ অনেক product, service বা item organize করে।
chapter project-এর মাধ্যমে এটি apply করুন: short brochure plan করুন, Photoshop-এ image prepare করুন, page-layout application-এ layout বানান এবং proof PDF তৈরি করুন।

Question: How do preflight, proofing, and a print-ready PDF reduce production errors?
Answer: English: Preflight checks fonts, images, links, color, bleed, page size, and output settings before print. Print proof provides a review copy for content, layout, color expectation, and finishing checks. Print-ready PDF follows the printer's requested standard, profile, marks, bleed, font, and image settings.
Run preflight, review a proof, and use the printer's requested PDF standard, profile, bleed, marks, and font settings instead of guessing them.
Bangla: Preflight print-এর আগে font, image, link, color, bleed, page size ও output setting check করে। Print proof content, layout, color expectation ও finishing check-এর review copy দেয়। Print-ready PDF printer-এর requested standard, profile, mark, bleed, font ও image setting অনুসরণ করে।
preflight চালান, proof review করুন এবং setting guess না করে printer-এর requested PDF standard, profile, bleed, mark ও font setting ব্যবহার করুন।

Question: What should Photoshop do, and what should a page-layout application do?
Answer: English: Photoshop role in brochures prepares, retouches, composites, and exports images but is not the strongest tool for long multi-page layout. Page-layout application uses tools such as InDesign or Affinity Publisher for text flow, styles, pages, links, and professional PDF output.
Prepare raster images in Photoshop, assemble flowing multi-page content in the page-layout application, and preflight the final PDF against the printer's specification.
Bangla: Photoshop role in brochures image prepare, retouch, composite ও export করে, কিন্তু long multi-page layout-এর strongest tool নয়। Page-layout application text flow, style, page, link ও professional PDF output-এর জন্য InDesign বা Affinity Publisher-এর মতো tool ব্যবহার করে।
Photoshop-এ raster image prepare করুন, page-layout application-এ flowing multi-page content assemble করুন এবং final PDF printer-এর specification অনুযায়ী preflight করুন।
