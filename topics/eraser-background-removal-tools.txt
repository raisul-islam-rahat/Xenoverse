Question: How are the Eraser, Background Eraser, and Magic Eraser different?
Answer: English: Eraser tool changes painted pixels to transparency or to the background color, depending on the layer. Background Eraser tool samples color under the pointer and erases matching background pixels as you brush. Magic Eraser tool removes connected or similar-colored pixels with one click based on its settings.
Practise it through the chapter task: remove a simple background once with an eraser and once with a layer mask, then compare how easily each result can be corrected.
Bangla: Eraser tool layer অনুযায়ী painted pixel-কে transparency বা background color-এ বদলায়। Background Eraser tool pointer-এর নিচের color sample করে এবং brush করার সময় matching background pixel erase করে। Magic Eraser tool setting অনুযায়ী এক click-এ connected বা similar-colored pixel সরায়।
chapter task-এর মাধ্যমে এটি practise করুন: একটি simple background একবার eraser এবং একবার layer mask দিয়ে সরান, তারপর কোন result সহজে ঠিক করা যায় তুলনা করুন।

Question: What is the difference between Continuous, Once, and Background Swatch sampling in Background Eraser?
