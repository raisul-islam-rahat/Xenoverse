Question: What is the difference between Skew, Distort, and Perspective transforms?
Answer: English: Skew slants selected content along an axis. Distort moves transform corners independently to reshape content. Perspective transform moves opposite transform corners in a linked way to suggest depth.
Test the options on duplicate layers with the same image, then compare control, edge quality, editability, and the final visible result.
Bangla: Skew selected content-কে একটি axis বরাবর কাত করে। Distort transform corner আলাদাভাবে সরিয়ে content reshape করে। Perspective transform depth বোঝাতে opposite transform corner যুক্তভাবে সরায়।
একই image-এর duplicate layer-এ optionগুলো test করুন; তারপর control, edge quality, editability ও final visible result compare করুন।

Question: Why should repeated transforms be applied to a Smart Object?
Answer: English: Transform on a Smart Object keeps repeated scaling and warping more editable than direct pixel transformation.
Use this as a workflow checkpoint before committing the edit. Watch the active layer, aspect ratio, and transform values, and avoid repeated destructive scaling of pixels.
Bangla: Transform on a Smart Object direct pixel transformation-এর তুলনায় repeated scaling ও warping বেশি editable রাখে।
edit commit করার আগে এটিকে workflow checkpoint হিসেবে ব্যবহার করুন। active layer, aspect ratio ও transform value দেখুন এবং pixel-কে বারবার destructively scale করবেন না।
