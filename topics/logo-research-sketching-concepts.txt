Question: What should a logo brief explain about the business goal and audience?
Answer: English: Logo brief defines the organization, goal, audience, personality, message, uses, limits, deliverables, and approval process. Business goal states what the identity should help the organization achieve. Logo audience identifies the people who must recognize, trust, or respond to the mark.
Apply it through the chapter project: write a real or fictional client brief, research the market, make a moodboard, draw at least twenty thumbnails, and develop three distinct concepts.
Bangla: Logo brief organization, goal, audience, personality, message, use, limit, deliverable ও approval process নির্ধারণ করে। Business goal identity organization-কে কী achieve করতে সাহায্য করবে তা জানায়। Logo audience যে মানুষ mark চিনবে, trust করবে বা respond করবে তাদের চিহ্নিত করে।
chapter project-এর মাধ্যমে এটি apply করুন: real বা fictional client brief লিখুন, market research করুন, moodboard বানান, কমপক্ষে বিশটি thumbnail আঁকুন এবং তিনটি distinct concept develop করুন।

Question: Why should I check possible trademark and cultural conflicts before approval?
Answer: English: Preliminary trademark search checks obvious conflicts in relevant markets but does not replace professional legal clearance. Cultural symbol research checks whether shapes, colors, words, or gestures carry unintended meanings in target cultures.
Document the markets and classes checked, show possible conflicts to the client, and obtain qualified legal clearance before claiming exclusive rights.
Bangla: Preliminary trademark search relevant market-এর obvious conflict দেখে, কিন্তু professional legal clearance-এর বিকল্প নয়। Cultural symbol research target culture-এ shape, color, word বা gesture-এর unintended meaning আছে কি না দেখে।
কোন market ও class check করা হয়েছে document করুন, possible conflict client-কে দেখান এবং exclusive right claim করার আগে qualified legal clearance নিন।
